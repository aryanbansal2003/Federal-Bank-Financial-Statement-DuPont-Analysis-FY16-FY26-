**Methodology**
Ratio |	Formula
Capital adequacy (CRAR) |	Tier 1 % + Tier 2 %
Debt-to-Equity |	(Deposits + Borrowings) ÷ Total shareholders' funds
CASA ratio |	(Current deposits + Savings deposits) ÷ Total deposits
Credit-Deposit ratio |	Advances ÷ Deposits
ROA |	Net profit ÷ Average total assets
ROE |	Net profit ÷ Average shareholders' funds
NIM |	(Total interest earned − Interest expended) ÷ Average interest-earning assets (balances with banks + investments + advances)
Advances / Deposit growth |	Current year ÷ Previous year − 1
Liquid assets ÷ Total assets |	(Cash with RBI + Balances with banks + Investments) ÷ Total assets
P/B ratio |	Market price ÷ Book value per share
DuPont ROE |	(Net profit ÷ Total income) × (Total income ÷ Average assets) × (Average assets ÷ Closing equity)

Techniques used: common-size analysis, comparative (year-on-year) analysis, ratio analysis, DuPont decomposition, peer benchmarking.
